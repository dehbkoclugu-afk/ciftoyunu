# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Users

Adults aged 18–34 who are together in person and want a better conversation: couples at different relationship stages, groups of two to eight friends, and creators using prompts as a social format.

## Product Purpose

Project Duo turns one shared phone into a paced deck of culturally adapted conversation cards. Success means people reach a first question in under a minute, put their attention back on each other, and complete meaningful sessions without creating an account.

## Positioning

The phone is only the deck. Offline-first sessions combine named-player prompts, a deliberate conversation arc, transparent content controls, and locally rewritten question catalogs instead of generic shuffled translations.

## Operating Context

The app is used during date nights, quiet evenings, cafés, trips, house parties, and creator recordings. Players pass one phone between them, answer aloud, and may skip any prompt without penalty.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Expo/React Native app for iOS, Android, and web development previews.
- Same-device couple and friend modes; no account or answer collection in V1.
- Offline embedded content, local history, favorites, session resume, local notifications, and RevenueCat premium access.
- English is canonical; Arabic, Japanese, Korean, and Traditional Chinese are Wave 1 release locales.
- `Project Duo` is a temporary code name. The final brand remains an open decision, so user-facing naming comes from configuration.
- V2 pairing, chat, cloud sync, public user-generated decks, and relationship scoring are excluded from V1.

## Brand Commitments

Warm, brave, premium, and playful without becoming childish, clinical, pornographic, or manipulative. The product never claims to provide therapy or repair a relationship. Debatium's product loop may inform mechanics, but its brand, copy, assets, screens, and question database are not copied.

## Evidence on Hand

The complete approved product contract is `docs/product/master-implementation-plan.md`. No final trademark-safe name, production illustration set, store credentials, prices, testimonials, or customer claims exist yet and none may be fabricated.

## Product Principles

1. Reach a real question in under 60 seconds.
2. Make the shared conversation—not the phone—the center of attention.
3. Show genuine free quality before asking for payment.
4. Adapt content culturally and ship only reviewed locale bundles.
5. Collect interaction telemetry, never player names or spoken answers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All core gestures have visible button alternatives; controls meet iOS 44 pt and Android 48 dp targets; content supports 200% text scaling, screen readers, reduced motion, light/dark appearance, and full RTL behavior.
